Details

2D Animation

Procreate Dreams

Resolution: 3840 × 2160 px
Duration: 0:39

About the Work

In this animation, a flour sack notices someone he finds beautiful and tries to get her attention. What begins as a hopeful attempt to impress her ends in disappointment when he discovers that she is a cardboard cutout.

The class assignment was to animate a flour sack performing an action. I developed this story and animated it in Procreate Dreams, practicing how to express personality and emotion through movement.

With such a simple character, the timing and gestures had to communicate his feelings. I wanted the ending to feel funny but also a little sad, turning a moment of excitement into a little heartbreak.
