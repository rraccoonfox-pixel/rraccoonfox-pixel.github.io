About the Work

These are two separate glitch experiments using photographs of myself. In Katana, I am wearing a red kimono and holding a katana. In Fallen Angel, I am dressed as an angel for Halloween.

For both works, I saved the photographs as raw data files in Photoshop, imported them into Audacity, and applied audio effects to the image data. I then reopened the altered files in Photoshop and saved the results. I wanted to explore how distorted colors, horizontal lines, and missing details could change familiar images of myself.
