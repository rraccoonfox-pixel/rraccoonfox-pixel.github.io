About the Work

Set in a dark kitchen at night, this short film introduces a mysterious creature that appears under the table and seems to love cookies. I wanted to turn an ordinary household moment into a playful story with suspense and an unexpected reveal.

I developed the idea, performed in the film, helped film the kitchen scenes, and edited the footage in Premiere Pro. I used camera angles, lighting, pacing, sound, and music to build anticipation and suggest that something was hiding nearby.

The opening nighttime clip comes from Pixabay. The remaining footage was filmed by Gleb Yeliseyev and me. Credits are included at the end of the film.
