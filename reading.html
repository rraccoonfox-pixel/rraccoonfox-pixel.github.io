About the Work

This video challenges viewers to keep reading as the words gradually speed up. The text presents interesting facts, while animated images, GIFs, and memes compete for the viewer's attention.

I created the video in After Effects, using images found through Google Images and synchronizing the animations with the music. The visuals make the challenge entertaining, while also becoming distractions that make it harder to read everything.

Viewers may experience the video differently on a second watch. The first time, they might focus on the animations or try to keep up with the words. Watching again gives them a chance to catch missed facts and notice more visual details. This is one of my favorite projects because it combines information, humor, music, and participation.
