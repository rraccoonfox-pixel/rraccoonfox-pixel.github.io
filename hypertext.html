About the Work

Are You Staying? is an interactive hypertext website about leaving, attachment, and deciding what to take or leave behind. For me, a suitcase does not always mean a happy trip. It can also bring feelings of uncertainty and remind me of leaving familiar places. I created this work because I think that whenever we leave, we leave something behind. An object, a person, or a part of our life.

I built the website in HTML, used simple CSS for the layout, and published it through GitHub Pages. I combined text and pictures with hyperlinks that let visitors choose what to pack, what to leave, and whether to move forward or go back. These choices shape the order in which they experience the story.

I wanted to turn my personal feelings into an experience that others could explore. There is no single path through the website, just as leaving does not feel the same for everyone. The work asks visitors to consider what matters to them and how it feels when they cannot take everything with them.
